# RECNOTE - PROJECT SCOPE & VISION

**Product Name**: Recnote
**Tagline**: "Record. Transcribe. Remember Everything."
**Mission**: Make video communication as simple as text

---

## THE BIG PICTURE

Recnote transforms how teams communicate by making video as simple as text. Right now, customer support agents spend hours typing explanations that customers still don't understand. Sales teams write long emails that get ignored. Product teams struggle to explain bugs with screenshots. Meetings are forgotten because notes are incomplete.

**Recnote changes this:** Record a 2-minute video showing exactly what you mean, get an AI transcript automatically, share it with a link, and let your team search through every word you've ever recorded. No downloads, no complexity, no compliance headaches—just hit record in your browser.

Start free, upgrade when you see the value. As we grow, we'll add enterprise features for legal depositions, HIPAA-compliant telemedicine, and regulated industries—but today, we're focused on making video communication so effortless that every support ticket, every sales pitch, every bug report, and every meeting becomes searchable, shareable, and unforgettable.

**The future of work is video-first, and Recnote makes that future accessible to everyone.**

## COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

### Direct Competitors

**Loom** ($975M acquisition by Atlassian)
- Strengths: Easy to use, great for async messaging
- Weaknesses: Not compliance-focused, expensive
- Our Advantage: Cheaper, AI-first, compliance-ready

**Vidyard**
- Strengths: Video for sales teams, analytics
- Weaknesses: Expensive, complex
- Our Advantage: Simpler, better pricing

**Riverside.fm**
- Strengths: High-quality podcast/video recording
- Weaknesses: Creator-focused, not for support teams
- Our Advantage: Different market, transcription-first

**Zoom**
- Strengths: Meetings, everyone uses it
- Weaknesses: Not built for recording/archiving
- Our Advantage: Purpose-built for recording + search

### Indirect Competitors

- **Support tools**: Zendesk, Intercom (no video focus)
- **Screen recorders**: Snagit, Camtasia (desktop apps, no cloud)
- **Legal video**: Legacy providers (expensive, poor UX)

**Our Positioning**: "Loom for customer support, with AI transcription"

## RISKS & MITIGATION

### Technical Risks

**Risk 1**: Browser compatibility issues
- **Mitigation**: Extensive testing, fallbacks, clear requirements

**Risk 2**: Large file handling (crashes, timeouts)
- **Mitigation**: Chunked uploads, compression, limits

**Risk 3**: AI transcription costs spiral
- **Mitigation**: Strict usage limits, efficient batching

### Business Risks

**Risk 4**: Nobody pays (no product-market fit)
- **Mitigation**: Validate with customers before building, iterate fast

**Risk 5**: Competitor launches similar product
- **Mitigation**: Move fast, build moat with compliance, lock in users

**Risk 6**: Cloudflare costs exceed budget
- **Mitigation**: Billing alerts, usage limits, monitor daily

### Market Risks

**Risk 7**: Market doesn't adopt video-first communication
- **Mitigation**: Unlikely (Loom proves demand), but have exit plan

**Risk 8**: Regulatory changes make compliance harder
- **Mitigation**: Start without compliance, add when we have revenue
